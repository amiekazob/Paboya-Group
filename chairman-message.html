Message from the Founder & Chairman

Paboya Group was founded with a simple belief: competitive industry transforms nations. We have invested in capabilities that raise quality, lower costs and build reliability for customers and communities.

Innovation drives our growth. We adopt technologies that improve productivity, safety and sustainability, while developing the talent that steers these systems.

Sustainability remains central to our operations. We optimize resources, reduce waste and uphold strict environmental standards across facilities.

We measure success by our contribution to national development. As we expand, we stay grounded in integrity, governance and service.

Samiul Alam Shajal
Founder & Chairman
